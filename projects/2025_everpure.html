Internship @ Everpure

May-Aug 2025 | ML, Python

Summer internship at Everpure. Trained an AI assistant for automated ticket classification and triage based on error logs. This project was in the early phases and involved heavy experimentation. As a result, I learned to adapt based on training results and pay close attention to any anomalous output, so that I could adjust model structure and data cleaning pipeline accordingly.

My main contributions:

  • Develop AI triaging assistant in Python, experimented with various ML/NLP libraries and techniques to improve assistant's training efficiency and overall performance.
  • Achieve F1-score of up to 77% on triaging assistant model.
