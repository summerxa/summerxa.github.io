The Weaver

GitHub | Play on Itch.io

May-Jun 2025 | Unity, C#, Game development

A 3D puzzle-adventure game, created by a team of 4 student developers. Built in 2 weeks for thatgamecompany x COREBLAZER GAME JAM 2025 The game jam theme was "Generosity". We ranked #33 out of 267 submissions by community vote.

For this game, I worked on a boolean puzzle where two puzzle pieces visually "cancel out" when overlapping; this effect was achieved using Unity shaders.

I also worked on adding VFX/particle effects to enhance the story. Whenever the player interacts with an NPC, they can choose to either take the NPC's memories or share their own memories with the NPC. If the player decides to take an NPC's memories, the NPC will dissolve into particles and fade away, giving the player's action a more heavy consequence. I worked on the VFX for this transition.
